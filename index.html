Our Mission

This resource is dedicated to cataloging and exploring the rapidly evolving intersection of Jewish tradition and artificial intelligence. As technology reshapes how we study texts, perform mitzvot, and understand our own humanity, we hope that this platform can serve as a central hub for teachers, scholars, rabbis, technologists, and the curious public.

Help Build This

This is a living project still in its early stages. We need rabbis, educators, AI researchers, and community members to help refine and expand it. Your expertise matters.
